THE CHALLENGE

Neighbors Bank is a national mortgage lender with 50+ underwriters handling USDA, FHA, VA, and conventional loans. Their team was wasting hours searching through shared drives and documents, with manual escalation processes via emails and manager tags. We built a custom Slack bot using RAG technology—underwriters ask questions in plain English and get instant answers from internal documents, with automated escalations and custom commands for advanced workflows.
