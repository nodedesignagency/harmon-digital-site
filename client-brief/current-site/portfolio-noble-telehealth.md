THE CHALLENGE

Noble TeleHealth needed a custom application to replace their manual booking and scheduling process. Patients and providers were stuck in a clunky workflow that led to missed appointments, double-bookings, and hours of administrative overhead. We built a custom healthcare application that handles the entire scheduling flow — patients book directly, providers manage availability, and the system handles confirmations and reminders automatically.
