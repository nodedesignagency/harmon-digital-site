## Who we are

Harmon Digital builds **custom software and AI employees for private equity portfolio companies.** Our goal is to grow EBITDA: cut software and fulfillment costs, and grow revenue with smart follow-up, inbound and outbound systems.

harmon-digital.com

The audience is PE operating partners and the leadership teams at their portcos. They think in EBITDA and multiples.

**The one goal of the site: get them to book a call.**

## What the homepage needs to roughly say (rough draft)

Roughly in this order. Use whatever template sections fit best.

1. **Hero:** - still thinking here
2. **Problem:** Rough brain dump outline: Your portcos are doing work that should be automated. Youre spending too much on software, leads are getting lost, calls not being followed up on or anwsered, guessing where you are with your portcos, data is not owned, employees are doing manuel repetitive work (data entry), you dont own your moat, you have to wait on new software features, You have to move fast but cant wait for external software to maybe add a feature. you're gluing 6 different softwares together. Your businesses is all over the place and or running legacy software.
3. **Solution:** We grow EBITDA by cutting software and fulfillment costs and increasing revenue with automated follow-up, inbound and outbound, and custom software moats.
4. **How it works:** We do a full Audit (visual maps and SOPs), Identify bottlenecks and oportunites, build, deploy and train, then keep improving.
5. **AI employees:** a ticker of the roles we set up (inbound responder, follow-up agent, outbound SDR, ops coordinator, back-office assistant, etc.).
6. **Portfolio dashboards:** a dashboard for every portco so the fund can see exactly where each company stands and exactly where the savings and revenue gains are coming from. This is a big selling point, so give it real visual weight.
7. **Who we've worked with:** client logos and case studies.
8. **Why us:** our internal platform, Unstack, speeds up development, and we can also build anything fully custom. and we have the experience.
9. **Before and after:** show how EBITDA changes before and after working with us. A static example is fine for now; it may become a calculator later.
10. **Training:** we train teams on Claude, ChatGPT and Unstack.
11. **Testimonials.**
12. **Pricing:** three tiers. Do it yourself (Unstack), Done with you (1:1 training), Done for you (custom builds).
13. **FAQ** and a final call to action.
